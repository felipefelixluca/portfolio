## Brief

The Customer Portal is one of MetLife Japan's central digital-transformation initiatives, focused on the **post-purchase experience**. Customers were buying policies through agents but rarely returning to the portal afterwards — and when they did, dense screens and an OTP-heavy login made high-intent tasks (changing a beneficiary, updating a billing preference, checking a contract) harder than they needed to be.

My role across the engagement: lead UX designer on the portal, with responsibility for research design, prototyping, hi-fi UI, and the cross-functional partnership that gets a design actually shipped.

## Process

We work inside a DevOps &amp; Scrum cadence. Rather than running a separate "design phase" off to the side, we fold research, prototyping, and validation **into the same sprint rhythm** as engineering — formative testing before development, summative testing after, and a kaizen loop on top.

The journey work below was the anchor artifact used in stakeholder conversations with Claims, Call Center, Customer Care, and IT. Mapping emotion and pain alongside channel and touchpoint forced specificity: instead of debating "the registration is hard," we were debating *which* OTP step was failing for *which* persona at *which* moment.

## Decisions

<Callout title="OTP stays, but only where the risk model demands it" accent="red">
We were under real pressure to remove OTP from registration entirely because of the drop-off it caused. Working with the security and compliance partners, we kept OTP at the **identity-binding moment** only and removed it from low-risk re-entry flows — preserving the security posture while cutting drop-off where it actually hurt.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Journey maps as a meeting format, not a deliverable" accent="yellow">
Adapted Value Proposition Canvas and journey mapping into short, in-the-flow working sessions for the BAU teams. The output was decisions in the room, not a 40-page artifact filed away — which is what made it usable by Claims and Call Center partners under their own delivery pressure.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Design system + design-to-code pilot" accent="blue">
Drove an internal design-to-code initiative (Figma MCP, Copilot, Claude Code) so component changes in Figma propagate to production-grade React without the usual translation lag. Built the onboarding programme and Power Automate intake workflow that standardised how requests flow into the team.
</Callout>

## Outcome

- **Registration flow** — +30% completion, 40% faster time-to-complete (2026).
- **Agent sales platform** (parallel programme I led) — 40% faster digital sales process, +11% completion on key procedures; contributed to MetLife Japan hitting its annual NPS targets (2025).
- **Operating model** — the team now serves Customer Portal, Operations Portal, and Producers Portal in parallel with the same cadence, supporting Claims, Call Center, Customer Care, and IT.
